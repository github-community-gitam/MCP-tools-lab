# Code of Conduct

MCP Tools Lab welcomes people of all experience levels and backgrounds.

- Be kind, patient, and respectful, especially when someone is learning.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about code rather than personal criticism.
- Respect different viewpoints and give credit for others' work.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, spam, or share private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation when needed.
For conduct concerns, contact a repository maintainer through a contact method they
have made available on their GitHub profile. Do not post private incident details in
public issues. GitHub's reporting tools are also available for platform abuse.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, and project discussions.
